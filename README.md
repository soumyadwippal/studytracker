Here are the main features of the **StudyFlow (Smart Study Tracker)** web page, explained simply so anyone can understand how to use them:

1. **Study Report**: This section gives you an overview of your past study sessions. You can use it to see how much time you've spent studying, track your overall progress, and analyze your productivity over time.

2. **Targets**: Here, you can set specific, short-term study targets (for example: "study for 2 hours today" or "read 3 chapters"). It helps you stay accountable and check off your daily or weekly achievements.

3. **Study Planner**: This acts as your schedule. You can use it to plan out your upcoming study sessions, organize which subjects or topics you need to tackle, and manage your time effectively.

4. **Goal Tracker**: This is for your big-picture ambitions (like "Pass my final exams" or "Learn a new language"). You can define large goals and break them down into smaller, manageable steps to stay motivated.

5. **Notes**: A dedicated space for you to jot down important information, summarize topics, or keep track of important concepts while you study, keeping everything centralized in one app.

Essentially, the app is designed to be a complete toolkit to help you plan what to study, track if you actually did it, monitor your long-term goals, and keep your notes all in one place!
